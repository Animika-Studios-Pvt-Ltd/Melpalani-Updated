Mathadhipathy

Om Sharavanabhava Matham was founded by Mathadhipathy Sri Sadhguru Sri Sharavana Baba, the divine incarnation of Lord Subrahmanya in the early 2000.

Babaji was born to humble parents Smt. Lakhsmi Amma and Shri. Krishnankutty Guptan on 11th October 1979 in the village of Sreekrishnapuram in Palakkad district of Kerala, India.

The first ashram was established in Kozhikode (Calicut) by devotees who benefitted from the grace of Babaji. Soon after that devotees established Om Sharavanabhava Dharma Trust, a charitable trust in the birthplace of Sreekrishnapuram, in Palakkad district of Kerala, India, to render charitable services to the needy.

Over a period of time, devotees from all walks to life, from all over the world visited Babaji and started centres / ashrams in their respective places, to serve humankind. Today, Babaji's mission has touched millions of lives all over the world.
